Antonius, a lean grey harbor seal with a faded red-and-white striped ruff collar at the neck, sharp intelligent eyes, and a deck of worn playing cards always near one flipper. Death, a tall gaunt figure in a heavy black hooded robe, a pale hollow face half lost in shadow beneath the hood, long white hands, and an utterly still, unhurried posture.

Wide-medium shot on a windswept headland at dusk: Antonius and Death seated across from one another on the grass at the cliff's edge, three cards laid between them, the sea pale and flat below. Low failing light, long shadows, static wide-normal lens.

black and white photograph, 1957 Swedish art cinema, deep shadows, natural light, 35mm film grain, photorealistic.
