# DET SJUNDE SÄLET
## (The Seventh Seal)
### A treatment. Black and white. Swedish with subtitles, even the parts set in Manhattan.

*Note on the title: in Swedish, Bergman's film is "Det sjunde inseglet" — the seventh seal of Revelation. Drop three letters and "det sjunde sälet" is, quite literally, the seventh seal, the animal. This film has been waiting to be made since 1957. Possibly since Revelation.*

---

## THE BROTHERS SÄL

A traveling circus works its way up the gray Baltic coast, drawing thinner crowds each season. Its entire bill: six seal brothers, sons of the legendary Ingmar Säl, who taught them that a seal balancing a ball on his nose is not an artist but a *collaborator*.

**ANTONIUS** — Sleight of flipper. Close-up work: coins, cards, herring produced from behind the ears of children. His hands, so to speak, are the fastest in Scandinavia. He has never been caught palming a card, chiefly because no one is certain he has palms.

**JÖNS** — The mentalist. Bitter, brilliant, reads minds nightly and has concluded from the evidence that minds are not worth reading. "Madam is thinking of the number seven. Madam is always thinking of the number seven. Madam should think bigger."

**SKAT** — Commedia dell'arte. Plays Arlecchino, Pantalone, and Il Dottore, sometimes in the same scene, the costume changes achieved by rotating in the water. Insufferable about his craft. Refers to the others' acts as "circus."

**PLOG** — Strongman and escapologist. Nightly he is chained, padlocked, and lowered into a tank of water, from which he escapes in under a minute — a feat somewhat diminished, Jöns observes, by the fact that he is a seal.

**RAVAL** — Ventriloquist. His dummy is a herring named Lilla Gunnar. The herring says the unspeakable things — about the audience, about the church, about their late father — while Raval's whiskers never move. The brothers suspect the herring has begun writing its own material.

**JOF** — The visionary. His act is invisible juggling: he juggles nothing, describes it rapturously, and the audience sees it or does not. He also has actual visions, which the brothers dismiss, because a prophet is never honored in his own circus.

And then there is the seventh.

**DR. S. SELKIRK** — born Sjunde Säl, the youngest, who shed the family act the way a selkie sheds its skin, swam to America, took a degree, and now keeps a private psychoanalytic practice on East 84th off Park. Heavy drapes. A good couch. He has not spoken to his brothers in eleven years. His patients notice nothing, or are too well-analyzed to say so.

---

## ACT ONE — THE STRANGER ON THE BEACH

Dawn. A stony shore. Antonius wakes from a bad dream to find a pale figure in black standing at the tideline, hourglass in hand.

**ANTONIUS:** Who are you?
**DEATH:** I am Death.
**ANTONIUS:** Have you come for me?
**DEATH:** I have long walked beside your circus. The attendance figures alone —

Antonius, thinking fast, proposes a game. Death, a traditionalist, suggests chess. There is an awkward interval regarding flippers. They settle on Three-card Monte. Death loses. Death loses *forty-one consecutive times*, because Antonius is palming the queen, and Death — who has seen everything but never actually watched closely — cannot prove it.

Enraged and, worse, *intrigued*, Death grants a stay: he will follow the circus north while the game continues, one hand per night.

---

## ACT TWO — THE CONFESSION

The famous confession scene, inverted. Death, seeking an edge, visits Jöns's mentalism tent disguised as a paying customer. But you cannot out-read the reader. Jöns peers at the hooded figure, goes very still, and announces to the crowd:

**JÖNS:** The gentleman is thinking that he has grown weary of his work. That he took the position eons ago because it was steady. That no one thanks him. That he has begun — the gentleman should stop me if I am wrong — to feel *nothing at all*.

Silence. Then, from beneath the hood, a small sound no one present has ever heard before: Death, sniffling.

Raval's herring, from offstage: **"Somebody get this man a therapist."**

Jöns knows just the seal.

---

## ACT THREE — EAST 84TH STREET

Death takes the couch. He is a difficult patient. He arrives precisely on time, which unnerves the doorman. He pays in obols. He resists the transference, then over-commits to it. Session notes, in Selkirk's careful hand:

*Patient presents with anhedonia, burnout, and an unexamined grandiosity ("I am the end of all things") masking profound loneliness. Patient cannot recall the last time anyone was happy to see him. Patient's relationship with his work is compulsive; he cannot stop "taking people," even on weekends. When I suggested a boundary, patient laughed for the first time. Duration of laugh: one hour. Billed accordingly.*

Weeks pass. Death cancels an appointment — *reschedules a death* — to attend a session. Somewhere in Belgium, a man who should have died choking on a rusk instead lives to ninety-four, bewildered.

But analysis cuts both ways. Death, on the couch, staring at the ceiling:

**DEATH:** And you, Doctor. Seven brothers. Six on a stage. One in this room. What do you call it when a seal will not perform?
**SELKIRK:** We call it individuation.
**DEATH:** Your father called it something else, I think.
**SELKIRK:** *(a long pause; he checks the clock)* We're at time.

---

## ACT FOUR — THE WILD STRAWBERRIES SCENE

The circus, meanwhile, has reached the northern coast, and word comes that Death's stay expires with the last hand of Monte. The brothers wire New York. Sjunde does not answer. Then he does. Then he is on a boat, then he is simply *swimming*, tie streaming behind him, because some things are faster than Cunard.

The reunion. Jof's wife sets out a bowl of cream and fresh herring on the rocks in the long northern twilight, and the seven brothers sit together for the first time in eleven years, and even Jöns is quiet.

**SELKIRK:** I shall remember this moment. The silence. The twilight. The bowl of herring. I shall carry it between my flippers like a bowl of fresh milk. It will be enough for me.

**RAVAL'S HERRING:** *(from the bowl, faintly)* Speak for yourself.

---

## ACT FIVE — THE LAST HAND

The final game, on the headland, at dusk. Death has been in treatment for six months and it shows: he hesitates. He asks Antonius how his week has been. He uses the phrase "sitting with that."

Antonius deals the last hand. And here Jof — who alone sees what others cannot — watches Death reach not for a card but for his hourglass, and finds it gone. Sleight of flipper. Antonius has palmed eternity itself and passed it flipper to flipper down the line of brothers, out to sea.

**DEATH:** That was not the game.
**ANTONIUS:** It was always the game.
**DEATH:** *(a long pause)* My analyst says I have difficulty with loss.

Death does not take them. Death, for the first time in the history of anything, *takes a sabbatical* — terminating treatment properly, as Dr. Selkirk insists, with a final session and appropriate closure.

---

## FINAL IMAGE

Dawn on the ridge above the sea. Jof, hushed, points: against the pale sky, a line of silhouettes — six seals undulating in solemn procession, and at their head the tall figure in black, all of them dancing away along the horizon in the danse macabre, except it is unmistakably a *conga line*, and Death, God help him, appears to be enjoying it.

Below, on the rocks, the seventh seal watches, then opens his notebook and begins the case study he can never publish.

*FIN.*

*(Fin. Obviously.)*

## THE PIPELINE

Everything below this line is about production, not story: how the treatment above
becomes 45 shots of stills, narration, and graded Ken Burns clips, cut together into
a rough cut — at zero cost, with every image generated by hand in a free web UI.

### Principles

- **No paid APIs, anywhere.** Narration is [edge-tts](https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts)
  (free, Microsoft). Image generation is manual — you paste prompts into a free
  generator (Gemini / Nano Banana) yourself; nothing in this repo calls an image API.
- **Never overwrite a generated artifact.** Takes and rough cuts get incrementing
  suffixes (`_take1`, `_take2`, ... / `_v1`, `_v2`, ...). Nothing in `takes/` or
  `build/` is ever clobbered by a re-run.
- **Fail gracefully, always.** A missing still or missing narration marks a shot
  `blocked` in the catalog. The build continues anyway, with a 2-second slate card
  (shot ID on black) standing in for whatever's missing. One bad shot never stops
  the rough cut from assembling.
- **Consistency lives in the prompts, not the model.** Every character's physical
  description is written once, under `anchors:` in `shots.yaml`, and spliced onto
  the front of every prompt that needs it by `emit_prompts.py`. Change an anchor
  once; every future prompt for that character picks it up.

### Project structure

```
seventh-seal/
├── config.yaml            # resolution, fps, tts engine/voice, grade parameters
├── shots.yaml              # the film: anchors + one entry per shot
├── prompts/                # generated: one .txt per shot, paste-ready
├── stills/
│   ├── raw/                # drop generated images here: <shot_id>_v1.png, _v2.png...
│   └── selected/            # the chosen take per shot, copied in by select.py
├── audio/
│   ├── narration/          # per-shot narration .wav, from narrate.py
│   └── music/              # public-domain tracks you supply (Bach; on brand)
├── takes/                  # per-shot rendered clips (still + Ken Burns + grade)
├── build/                  # assembled cuts: rough_cut_v1.mp4, rough_cut_v1.edl.txt, ...
├── scripts/                # the pipeline itself (see below)
├── tests/                  # timing-math unit tests for render_shot.py / assemble.py
├── CATALOG.md              # regenerated by status.py --write
└── README.md               # this file
```

### Config

`config.yaml` is the one file you edit to change global behavior:

- **Narration engine**: `tts.engine: edge_tts` (default, free) or `tts.engine: openai`.
  Flipping this one value, once an OpenAI-backed narrate path exists, switches the
  whole pipeline to `tts.openai.model: tts-1-hd` / `tts.openai.voice: onyx` — no
  script changes needed, `narrate.py` just dispatches on `tts.engine`.
- **Monochrome grade**: `grade.saturation`, `grade.contrast`, `grade.vignette*` —
  baked into every take by `render_shot.py`'s ffmpeg filter chain. Desaturation +
  a slight contrast lift + a gentle vignette. Nothing else; no other stylization.
- **Ken Burns**: `motion.ease` (only `sine_in_out` for now — eased in and out, never
  linear) and `motion.supersample` (zoompan quality/speed tradeoff).
- **Crossfade**: `crossfade_frames` (default 12) — the default transition between
  shots; a shot can override it with `cut: hard` in `shots.yaml`.
- **Ingest**: `ingest.min_long_edge_px` (default 2200) — stills below this trigger a
  warning (not a rejection) from `ingest.py`, since the Ken Burns push could upscale.

### The working loop

```
python scripts/status.py                       # see what the film needs
python scripts/emit_prompts.py                  # write/refresh paste-ready prompts
                                                 #   for shots without a selected still
# ... paste each prompts/<shot_id>.txt into the free generator, save results into
# ... stills/raw/ as <shot_id>_v1.png, _v2.png, ... (one file per take)
python scripts/ingest.py                        # validate + register new stills
python scripts/select.py <shot_id> <version>    # pick the winning take per shot
python scripts/narrate.py                       # shots.yaml narration -> audio/narration/*.wav
python scripts/render_shot.py --all-ready       # render graded Ken Burns clips for
                                                 #   every selected still missing a take
python scripts/assemble.py                      # rough cut from whatever exists,
                                                 #   slates standing in for the gaps
```

Repeat daily as free credits allow. Work `production_priority: high` shots first —
they're the calibration shots (first appearance of each principal character, the
big set-pieces) that establish the look before you burn generator credits on
everything else.

`python scripts/status.py --write` regenerates `CATALOG.md` any time you want a
human-readable snapshot of the whole film's state without touching the pipeline.

### Shot states

Every shot is always in exactly one of five states, computed live (never cached)
from `shots.yaml`, `select.json`, and the filesystem:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `needs_prompt` | `emit_prompts.py` hasn't written this shot's prompt yet |
| `awaiting_still` | prompt exists, nothing ingested in `stills/raw/` for it yet |
| `needs_select` | stills ingested, none chosen with `select.py` yet |
| `blocked` | a selection exists but its file is missing, or narration text is set but the narration `.wav` hasn't been generated |
| `ready` | selected still on disk, and narration (if any) on disk — safe to render |

### Scripts

- **`emit_prompts.py`** — `shots.yaml` → `prompts/*.txt`. Splices each shot's
  character anchors + scene description + the global style suffix into one
  paste-ready block. By default skips shots that already have a selected still;
  `--force` regenerates everything, `--shot <id>` targets one shot regardless.
- **`ingest.py`** — scans `stills/raw/`, validates the `<shot_id>_v<N>.<ext>` naming
  convention, checks minimum resolution, and records results in a small local
  cache (`stills/raw/.ingest_catalog.json`) so unchanged files aren't re-validated.
  A bad filename, an unknown shot id, or an unreadable image is reported and
  skipped — never crashes the whole scan.
- **`select.py <shot_id> <version>`** — marks a take as the winner, copies it into
  `stills/selected/`, and records the choice in `select.json`.
- **`narrate.py`** — `shots.yaml` narration text → `audio/narration/*.wav` via
  edge-tts (or OpenAI tts-1-hd/onyx, once wired up, by flipping `tts.engine`).
- **`render_shot.py`** — one selected still → one graded Ken Burns clip in `takes/`.
  If the shot's narration runs longer than its `shots.yaml` duration, the render
  extends to `narration_length + 0.5s` and logs the override rather than clipping
  the audio. Never overwrites — each render gets the next `_takeN` suffix.
- **`assemble.py`** — concatenates the selected takes in `publish_order`, crossfades
  12 frames between shots (or hard-cuts where `shots.yaml` sets `cut: hard`), mixes
  in narration and music, and inserts a 2-second slate card for any shot that isn't
  `ready`. Also writes a plain-text EDL alongside the cut: shot id, in/out
  timecodes, and audio sources for every cut.
- **`status.py`** — prints the table above; `--write` regenerates `CATALOG.md`.

### Tests

`tests/` covers the timing math only — the arithmetic that decides how long a
render actually is and where every cut falls — since that's the part that's both
non-trivial and silent to get wrong:

```
python -m pytest tests/
```
